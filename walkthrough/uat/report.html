Findings

F1Signing secrets never leave the origin replica (D5): actors must be provisioned on their native machine; a directory-synced actor is verify-only elsewhere. Caught live in run 1 (transcripts kept). Backlog: actor provisioning for a second machine.process
F2Self-approval not blocked. bob submitted and approved WT-2 himself → done. The gate enforces the review step, not the reviewer. PRD says agents can't self-approve. P1 for next sprint: reject approval when the approver is an agent who authored the submission.product gap
F3Claims made concurrently before any sync both succeed locally and resolve at merge (D8 first-wins, proven in sprint-01 U3) — but the loser learns only from the log. Suggest a merge-time notice.ux
